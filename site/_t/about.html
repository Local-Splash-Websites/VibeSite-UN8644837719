About Odd Job Pros LLC

Odd Job Pros LLC provides dependable handyman and repair services throughout Piedmont, South Carolina, helping customers maintain safe, functional, and well-kept properties.

Who We Are 

Reliable Repairs With Local Experience

Are you dealing with unfinished repairs, unreliable contractors, or a growing list of projects around your home or business? At Odd Job Pros LLC in Piedmont, SC, we provide dependable handyman services built around clear communication, fair pricing, and quality workmanship. We are a military veteran-owned business with more than 25 years of experience serving residential and commercial customers. As a fully insured local handyman, we believe no job is too small, and we approach every project with the same level of care and accountability. Our mission is simple: get the job done right the first time while providing straightforward, reliable, and affordable home repair services with no hidden fees. Our slogan says it best: “I say what I do, and I do what I say.”

As an experienced handyman contractor, we handle a wide range of home maintenance services and general handyman services for properties of all sizes. Our residential handyman work includes furniture assembly, drywall repair, door repair, window repair, deck repair, fence repair, painting services, plumbing repair, and electrical repair. We also help customers reclaim usable space through junk removal, garage organizer projects, and practical garage organizing solutions. For property owners, managers, and investors, our commercial handyman services can help keep buildings functional, presentable, and ready for tenants, employees, or customers. Whether you need a single repair completed or several maintenance projects handled efficiently, we focus on practical solutions and dependable results. We also provide apartment turns, property turns, Airbnb turns, Airbnb services, and rental home turns to help owners prepare properties between occupants or guests. Our experience allows us to address repair, maintenance, cleanup, and improvement needs without making the process more complicated than it needs to be. We can also assist with hardscape projects and other odd jobs that may not fit neatly into one category.

With 25+ years of experience, full insurance, veteran-owned values, and a commitment to keeping our word, we make it easier to get important work off your list. For dependable service (no job too small), contact Odd Job Pros LLC in Piedmont, SC, and tell us what you need done.
