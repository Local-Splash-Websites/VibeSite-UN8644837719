Privacy Policy

The Company, Odd Job Pros LLC , values the privacy of our customers and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information obtained from The Customer. This policy also complies with all state and federal laws in the United States regarding the collection, use, and protection of personal information.


By using the website, oddjobprosllc.com, The Customer agrees to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If The Customer does not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use our website.

Information Collection

The Company may collect personal information from The Customer when they provide it voluntarily on our website, such as filling out a form or subscribing to a newsletter. We may collect non-personal information, such as browser type, IP address, and referring site.

Information Use

The Company may use the information collected to

  • Improve our website and services.
  • Communicate with The Customer and respond to inquiries.
  • Advertise to The Customer through Facebook Ads with Meta.
  • Provide requested services or information.

SMS Communications

By submitting a form on this website, The Customer agrees to receive text messages from The Company related to inquiries, services, appointments, and other business communications. Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply.


The Customer may opt out of receiving text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchase.


The Company does not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers or SMS consent information with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

Information Sharing

The Company may share The Customer's information with trusted third parties, such as Facebook and its affiliates, to facilitate Facebook Ads with Meta. We may also share information with service providers who assist us in operating our website and conducting business activities, such as payment processing or shipping. We will not sell, rent, or trade The Customer's information to any third party for marketing purposes without their explicit consent.


We may also disclose personal information in response to legal requests, such as subpoenas or court orders, or to comply with applicable laws, regulations, legal processes, or government requests.

Information Security

The Company takes reasonable measures to protect The Customer's information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. We use SSL encryption technology to protect personal information transmitted over the internet.

However, please be aware that no security measures are 100% effective, and we cannot guarantee the security of The Customer's information.

Data Retention

We will retain The Customer's personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law

Changes to this Privacy Policy

The Company may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, and any changes will be reflected on our website. The Customer is encouraged to periodically review this Privacy Policy to stay informed about how we collect, use, and protect their information.

Contact
